What Is Four Square?

This page is designed for absolute beginners. If you’ve never played Four Square before—or you’ve only seen it on a playground—this guide will quickly explain what the game is, how it’s played at a basic level, and why people of all ages love it.

If you’re looking for official rules, competitive standards, or World Championship gameplay, you’ll want to visit our Rules of Four Square page, which goes into full detail.
