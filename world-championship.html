WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP

Where the world’s best square off!
🏆

Welcome to the official home of the Four Square World Championship — the premier annual competition where the best square ballers from around the world battle for glory. This is where legends are made, rivalries are settled, and the energy is unmatched. Whether you’re playing or spectating, you’re part of the action.

Four Square isn’t just a playground pastime — it’s one of the most underrated sports in the world, blending athleticism, agility, lightning-quick reflexes, anticipation, and sharp strategy. Every rally is a test of eye-hand coordination and creativity, where a single move can shift the momentum of the game. What makes it truly special is its accessibility: anyone, anywhere, can step onto a court with just a ball and a few squares drawn on the ground. From casual neighborhood games to intense championship showdowns, Four Square bridges generations and skill levels like no other sport.

The World Championship is more than a tournament — it’s a celebration of community and competition. Players and fans alike come together from across the globe, united by a shared love of the game. Here, the stakes are high as we determine who will rise to the top and claim the title of world’s best, but just as important are the friendships forged, the stories shared, and the collective passion that fills the air. Whether you’re chasing the crown or cheering from the sidelines, you’ll leave inspired, connected, and ready to play again.

🌍 About the Championship

Since 2005, Bridgton Recreation in Maine has hosted the Four Square World Championships.

Entrance to the Championship is open to the public, so expect people of all shapes, sizes, and stature in attendance. Competitors will battle for the greatest four square title in the world — and don’t be shy! Feel free to wear something that represents where you’re from or visit the MERCH page for official swag.

📚 Championship History

From 2005–2022, Squarefour.org covered all things related to the World Championship. For past champions and archived coverage, visit Squarefour.org.

Since 2023, this site has taken over as the home for not only the World Championship but also as the central one-stop shop for all things Four Square.
