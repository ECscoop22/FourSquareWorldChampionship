Can a player hit the ball twice?

From: Alex – New York, NY

Question: Is it allowed to hit the ball twice during a turn?

Answer: Great question! Players may only hit the ball once per turn. Hitting twice results in an out.

Published: October 7, 2025

What’s the official Four Square ball size?

From: Sam – Boston, MA

Question: I’m curious about the official ball size for tournaments.

Answer: The official ball for championship play is a standard playground rubber ball, size 5. This ensures consistent bounce and gameplay.

Published: October 1, 2025
